# Related work

Public repositories that informed the fixture and the evaluator:

- Synthetic corpora with evidence-level gold and paired comparisons, as in ObsidianRAG.
- Qrels, language-separated reporting, and lexical, dense, and reranked baselines, as in MIRACL, MTEB, and BEIR.
- A clear split between product documentation and reproduction material, as in LightRAG and GraphRAG.

Automatic LLM judges are not primary evidence for this repository. Deterministic rank and evidence metrics are.
